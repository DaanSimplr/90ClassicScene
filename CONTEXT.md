## What the site is

- A showcase site about Japanese sports cars.
- Scope: a broad overview, not limited to one car or one scene.
- Language: English.
- Name: Shinyaso (logo: SHIN YASŌ, 深夜走). Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-15.
- Built with Astro as a static site, hosted on Vercel (free Hobby plan, which is for non-commercial use). Hosting chosen by the owner on 2026-11-15.
- The footer advertises that the owner builds websites ("Like this website? Let me build one for you!"). Vercel's Hobby terms allow only "personal or non-commercial use" and do not define commercial; the owner chose on 2026-11-23 to add the line anyway and accept the risk.
- Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics. Added by the owner on 2026-11-15.
- Email alerts are sent with EmailOctopus (double opt-in; list stored in the EU). Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-15.

## Who it serves

- Existing enthusiasts: people who already know the cars and want depth, not a beginner's introduction.

## What it must contain

- **Model pages:** one per car, with history and specs.
- **Brand and era overviews.**
- **Photo gallery.**
- **Articles or deep dives.**

## Coverage at launch

- Four named models:
  - Mitsubishi 3000GT
  - Toyota MR2
  - Honda Integra
  - Nissan Silvia
- All major brands: Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Subaru.
- 90s icons such as the Supra, Skyline GT-R, RX-7 and NSX.

## What a visitor should do

1. Browse and read the model pages, overviews, gallery and articles.
2. Subscribe by email to alerts when new content goes live. Alerts only, not a regular newsletter.

- Dark, neon, night-city mood: dark backgrounds with neon accents.
